Her Journey: Living with Vascular Dementia & Alzheimer's

Mum spent her whole life serving our family and church. When her memory started fading and she began having falls, leaving my job to care for her at home was an honour I didn't take lightly. Our days are grounded in patience, routine, and old Samoan choir hymns in the afternoon.
